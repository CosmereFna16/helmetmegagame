Centuries of oppression, darkness, and humiliation. Humankind was windwhipped on the crags of the wet but barren Earth, like animals.

Who brought this upon us? Who cursed us? Was it ourselves or someone else, something greater?

Fortunately, we have the Lifeweb now. It was built a long time ago. Now it's the year of our Lord God, 1210. There are other places on this Earth, or perhaps in space, where people live happily. But we only know Ravenheart. Ravenheart is kept alive by the Lifeweb, which in turn is fed by the blood of Ravenheart's people. The Lifeweb protects Ravenheart from the stormy, windy wastelands outside, making it so people can breathe, farm, and build. Ravenheart is hospitable and enviable, but it's a fragile place, a glimmer of order in a sea of inscrutability...
