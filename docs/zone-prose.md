## Town

Ants in the marshy chaff. The town is surrounded by an old wall of ancient ceramide, so old it looks exhumed from the ground, patched by wooden palisade. Decayed electric lights illuminate the narrow streets. Speakers blare the Baron's words. It is claustrophobic—salt-of-the-earth, virtuous, and degenerate types all in one disease-ridden congregation.

## Fortress

The fortress and its small surrounding estates rise nobly from the wooded hills, the keep itself visible behind the walls. They look down upon the town. A stone bridge fords the river.

The Lifeweb emits primordial green fires.

### Keep

Stone walls reinforced with ceramite, the color of terracotta and steel, draped with the Baron's fluttering heraldry. Old electric lamps of various colors illuminate the warm, comfortable interiors.

The gates open into a hallway branching into the Keep's many rooms. Past gargoyle-studded columns, the throne room. Stairs lead downwards into the Undercroft and upwards into the Quarters.

#### Throne Room

Intimately small, with an ornately carved wooden throne. Benches form concentric circles near the entrance. There's an elevated arcade to the left, and a hearth to the right.

#### Great Hall

The roof vaults over rows of banqueted tables. There's a balcony; the view plunges downwards into the forest.

#### Council Room

A wide table. There's a holographic map of Ravenheart and a button for the intercom.

#### The Charon

A sealed gate. The Baron's key opens this.

#### Swimming Pool

An extravagant design. Its novelty has worn off, and now it's a place where the nobility fall in and drown.

### Gatehouse

Grass rises rebelliously around the manicured paths, illuminated by searchlight. Past the portcullis, old stone stairs rise towards the Keep, and further yet, to the Lifeweb tower. The Gatehouse itself has several watchtowers and a murder hole over the gate. A winch system controls the portcullis. There is a back gate behind the fortress leading into the forest, but it's equally guarded.

There is a triple-barreled anti-air autocannon in the center of the yard. It seems to be off.

#### Gate

##### Merchant's Depot

Inside the caves, crates around a decayed metal frame. There is a zig-zag gap in the roof where light comes in. Besides the shuttle pad, there is a ledger room and a bunkhouse. Monsters regularly approach the depot.

### Railroad

A powered train station looming over an inscrutable abyss. You could get on, but where would you go? There are openings in the subway tunnels leading into ancient, unused maintenance areas. Where the tunnels break into open air, you can indeed see the pits, deformed and warped into unnatural stone. There's reflective metal down there. Chrome? Ceramite?

### Aberrant Pits

The start and end of all things. What madness brought you down here? The stone curves into strange, meandering passageways, but ceramite catches your torchlight. Monuments of chrome—cities within the deep.
